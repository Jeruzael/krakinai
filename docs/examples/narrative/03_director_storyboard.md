# 🎬 03 Director's Storyboard: Wool and Fang: The Snare in the Timberline

**Project Metadata:**
* **Target Engine:** Google Flow Omni (Strict 5–10s Clip Window)
* **Visual Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (2.39:1 Anamorphic Presentation)
* **Cinematic Aesthetic:** 35mm anamorphic dark fantasy realism; Alexa Mini LF tone curve; rich organic film grain; tangible fur, wool, and wrought iron textures.
* **Spatial Anchor:** Meadowbrook Valley rim and Whispering Pine Woods. Northern ridge with ancient knotted oak and hunter's tension snare; southern rolling orchards and fortified stone village square with garrison gate.
