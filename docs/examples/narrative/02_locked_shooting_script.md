## Locked Scene & Beat Breakdown

### SCENE 1: EXT. MEADOWBROOK VILLAGE OUTSKIRTS & WHISPERING PINES - DAWN

#### `[BEAT-01.1]` `[Est: 6s]` — Shadows on the Ridge (Establishing Staging)
- **Visual Action:** Extreme wide establishing shot tracking over the pine timberline. Dense morning mist clings to the tree line as pale dawn light illuminates the pastoral valley below: humanoid sheep in linen aprons tending orchards around stone cottages.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Ambient early morning wind, distant orchard rooster crow)*

#### `[BEAT-01.2]` `[Est: 8s]` — The Alpha's Injunction
- **Visual Action:** Medium two-shot inside the thorny brush. KAEL crouches beside the hulking, scarred alpha VANE. Vane leans into Kael's ear, extending a scarred claw toward the eastern perimeter fence.
- **Dialogue:**
  **VANE**
  *(predatory/ruthless, conspiratorial whisper, low raspy growl, baring yellowed fangs while claw pointing east)*
  "Walang bantay sa silangang bakod. Pagbilang ko ng tatlo, sunggaban n'yo agad 'yung mga nasa bungad. Walang matitira."

#### `[BEAT-01.3]` `[Est: 6s]` — The Wolf's Hesitation
- **Visual Action:** Tight close-up on Kael. His amber eyes widen with suppressed reluctance; his claws dig deep into the damp black loam as his hollow stomach spasms with hunger.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Low guttural breath, wet earth crunching under claw)*

---

#### `[BEAT-02.1]` `[Est: 6s]` — The Wolf Pack Breaches
- **Visual Action:** Wide dynamic track. Vane throws his head back and unleashes a blood-curdling howl. The five wolf raiders explode from the brush, vaulting over the low mossy fieldstone wall into the village outer ring.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Resonant piercing wolf howl, gravel scattering)*

#### `[BEAT-02.2]` `[Est: 6s]` — Alarm in the Square
- **Visual Action:** High-angle crane shot overlooking Meadowbrook market square. The bronze watchtower bell clangs violently. Sheep villagers drop wooden apple baskets, bleating in panic and scrambling toward heavy timber cellar doors.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Clanging alarm bells, frantic bleating and clattering wooden crates)*

#### `[BEAT-02.3]` `[Est: 7s]` — The Healer's Courage
- **Visual Action:** Ground-level dolly shot. ELOWEN slides across wet cobblestones, hoisting a weeping lamb to his feet and shoving him through a cottage door just as Kael's towering shadow falls across the stone alley.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Desperate child whimpers, door slamming shut, heavy predatory footstep)*

---

#### `[BEAT-03.1]` `[Est: 6s]` — Garrison Gates Breach
- **Visual Action:** Low-angle wide shot. The reinforced iron-banded gates of the village garrison smash open with explosive force. Splinters and stone dust erupt into the morning air.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Heavy timber splintering, resonant ground tremor)*

#### `[BEAT-03.2]` `[Est: 8s]` — The Vanguard's Challenge
- **Visual Action:** Medium shot of the three massive humanoid Mastiff Dogs stepping through the smoke: GARRICK, BRUTUS, and REX in spiked iron collars. Garrick steps forward, slamming his articulated steel gauntlets together with a deafening ring.
- **Dialogue:**
  **GARRICK**
  *(dominant/mocking, booming projection, deep boulder-grinding rumble, slamming iron-plated gauntlets together)*
  "Akala n'yo ba puro tupa lang ang madadatnan n'yo rito? Mga hangal!"

#### `[BEAT-03.3]` `[Est: 6s]` — The Alpha's Shock
- **Visual Action:** Over-the-shoulder shot behind Garrick facing Vane. Vane skids to a halt on the cobblestones, his claws leaving scrape marks on the wet pavers as his ears pin back in horror.
- **Dialogue:**
  **VANE**
  *(shocked/alarmed, choked gasp, ragged strained voice, flattening ears and skidding backward across the stones)*
  "Mga Aso?! Paanong nagkaroon ng Vanguard dito—?!"

---

#### `[BEAT-04.1]` `[Est: 7s]` — The Vanguard Clashes
- **Visual Action:** Dynamic tracking action shot. Brutus drives his iron shoulder through two leaping wolves, completely demolishing an apple wagon. Rex clamps his jaws onto a raider's leather armor, hurling him into the mud.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Crashing timber wagon, canine snarling, heavy mud splash)*

#### `[BEAT-04.2]` `[Est: 7s]` — The Iron Backhand
- **Visual Action:** Slow-motion medium close-up. Kael lunges forward with claws bared to guard his packmates. Garrick bats Kael's claws aside with his left arm and drives a brutal iron-plated backhand across Kael's rib cage, sending him crashing into the mud.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Brutal metallic crack, breath bursting from Kael's lungs, wet impact)*

#### `[BEAT-04.3]` `[Est: 6s]` — The Retreat Call
- **Visual Action:** Tight tracking shot of Vane scrambling backward into the fog, blood dripping from his brow as he turns to sprint toward the woods.
- **Dialogue:**
  **VANE**
  *(panicked/frantic, barking shout, breathless gravelly screech, clutching bleeding brow while scrambling in retreat)*
  "Umatras na tayo! Masyado silang malakas! Takbo!"

---

#### `[BEAT-05.1]` `[Est: 6s]` — Limping Rearguard
- **Visual Action:** Tracking shot through dense pine mist. Kael brings up the rear of the retreating pack, clutching his burning ribs with one claw and limping heavily to draw pursuit away from the younger wolves.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Ragged panting, heavy limping strides through pine needles)*

#### `[BEAT-05.2]` `[Est: 7s]` — The Snare Springs
- **Visual Action:** Wide tracking profile. Kael vaults a mossy fallen hemlock. *SNAP!* A hidden steel-reinforced hemp snare whips around his right ankle. A bent sapling counterweight recoils upward, yanking him into the air and slamming him down against the roots of an ancient oak.
- **Dialogue:**
  **KAEL**
  *(agonized/desperate, strained cry, breathless rasping shriek, clawing at the pine needles while thrashing pinned leg)*
  "Agh! Vane! Tulong!"

#### `[BEAT-05.3]` `[Est: 6s]` — Betrayal in the Mist
- **Visual Action:** Point-of-view shot from Kael's grounded perspective. Fifty yards ahead in the drifting fog, Vane stops, looks back at Kael thrashing in the rope, catches sight of approaching torchlight, and turns to bolt into the dark pines.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Distant dog horns blowing, torchlight flickering through fog)*

---

#### `[BEAT-06.1]` `[Est: 6s]` — Strangled Ankle
- **Visual Action:** Tight macro shot. Kael's bloody claws furiously rake at the tight hemp noose, but the knot cinches deeper into his silver ankle fur, dark crimson seeping down onto the tree root. Exhausted, he slumps against the coarse bark.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Strained rasping breaths, rope fibers tightening with creaks)*

#### `[BEAT-06.2]` `[Est: 7s]` — The Defiant Crouch
- **Visual Action:** Low medium shot. A sharp snap of a twig rings out ten paces away. Kael's ears snap erect, and he forces his battered body into a 3-point defensive tripod crouch, baring his bloody fangs toward the mist.
- **Dialogue:**
  **KAEL**
  *(defiant/cornered, ragged snarl, raspy guttural tremble, baring bloody fangs while bracing back against the tree)*
  "Lumapit ka na... tapusin mo na 'to!"

---

#### `[BEAT-07.1]` `[Est: 6s]` — The Healer Appears
- **Visual Action:** Medium shot through dew-covered bracken ferns. ELOWEN parts the fronds, clutching her woven herb basket. She freezes, her wide amber-hazel eyes shifting from Kael's menacing fangs down to the bloody snare cutting into his leg.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Soft rustling ferns, dripping water droplets)*

#### `[BEAT-07.2]` `[Est: 6s]` — The Accusation
- **Visual Action:** Reverse medium shot over Kael's shoulder looking at Elowen. She takes a cautious half-step back, clutching her herbal satchel tightly to her chest.
- **Dialogue:**
  **ELOWEN**
  *(fearful/tentative, hesitant whisper, soft trembling breath, clutching satchel tightly while taking a cautious half-step back)*
  "Isa ka sa mga... umatake kanina."

#### `[BEAT-07.3]` `[Est: 7s]` — Wounded Pride
- **Visual Action:** Close-up on Kael. His bloody jaw tightens; he turns his head aside toward the mossy bark in bitter, agonizing pride.
- **Dialogue:**
  **KAEL**
  *(bitter/resigned, clipped defensive snarl, dry hoarse growl, clenching bloody jaw and turning eyes away in wounded pride)*
  "Ano pang hinihintay mo? Tawagin mo na 'yung mga bantay n'yo. Sigaw na!"

---

#### `[BEAT-08.1]` `[Est: 6s]` — The Hound's Cry
- **Visual Action:** Low-angle shot of Elowen glancing back downhill. Far through the mist, flickering orange torchlight reflects against the pines as Garrick's deep bark echoes up the slope.
- **Dialogue:**
  **GARRICK (O.S.)**
  *(commanding/relentless, distant booming bellow, gravelly resonance, shouting down the pine slope)*
  "Suyurin ang gubat! Siguraduhing walang natirang lobo!"

#### `[BEAT-08.2]` `[Est: 7s]` — The Sickle Drawn
- **Visual Action:** Two-shot profile. Elowen reaches into her healer's satchel and draws a curved iron harvesting sickle. Seeing the steel catch the morning light, Kael squeezes his eyes shut, bracing his neck for the death blow.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Sharp metallic chime of sickle blade, Kael's sharp intake of breath)*

#### `[BEAT-08.3]` `[Est: 7s]` — Unexpected Mercy
- **Visual Action:** Close ground shot. Elowen drops to her knees in the damp moss beside Kael. Her small, trembling hands grasp the taught hemp cord away from his shredded fur and begin sawing frantically with the curved blade.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Heavy rhythmic sawing sound, soft rustle of linen and wool against moss)*

---

#### `[BEAT-09.1]` `[Est: 6s]` — The Wolf's Disbelief
- **Visual Action:** Over-the-shoulder tight shot looking down past Kael's muzzle at Elowen's cream curls. Kael's eyes pop open in utter shock as he watches her wrists work.
- **Dialogue:**
  **KAEL**
  *(disbelieving/bewildered, breathy whisper, strained husky gasp, staring wide-eyed at her hands)*
  "A-anong ginagawa mo? Nababaliw ka na ba? Lobo ako!"

#### `[BEAT-09.2]` `[Est: 9s]` — The Healer's Conviction
- **Visual Action:** Low-angle close-up on Elowen's face. Sweat and dew glisten on her forehead; her warm wool brushes against Kael's fur as she saws with all her strength against the tough strands.
- **Dialogue:**
  **ELOWEN**
  *(urgent/resolute, breathless hiss, panting shaky voice, sawing furiously with sickle as wool brushes against him)*
  "Alam ko! Pero kapag naabutan ka nila Garrick dito, papatayin ka nila nang walang kalaban-laban!"

#### `[BEAT-09.3]` `[Est: 6s]` — The Snare Parted
- **Visual Action:** Macro shot of the rope. *SHHK!* The last hemp strand splits under the sickle edge. The tension releases violently; the severed rope whips upward into the oak branches, freeing Kael's bloodied paw.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Sharp snap of rope, high-velocity whip through pine branches)*

---

#### `[BEAT-10.1]` `[Est: 6s]` — Fragile Standoff
- **Visual Action:** Eye-level two-shot. Kael scrambles backward and stands to his full height, testing his bruised ankle against the moss. Elowen remains kneeling on the forest floor, holding the bloody sickle loosely in her lap, looking up with complete vulnerability.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Heavy silence, distant snapping branches thirty yards downhill)*

#### `[BEAT-10.2]` `[Est: 6s]` — The Urge to Flee
- **Visual Action:** Close-up on Elowen kneeling. She urgently gestures with both hands toward the deeper mountain pass.
- **Dialogue:**
  **ELOWEN**
  *(frantic/protective, urgent whisper, rapid breathy cadence, waving trembling hands to push him away)*
  "Umalis ka na! Bilis, bago pa sila makarating dito!"

#### `[BEAT-10.3]` `[Est: 6s]` — The Vow in the Pines
- **Visual Action:** Close-up on Kael's face framed against dark evergreen boughs. His amber eyes soften with profound gratitude; he lowers his chin in a respectful nod.
- **Dialogue:**
  **KAEL**
  *(reverent/solemn, quiet murmur, husky raw tone, locking amber eyes with hers and bowing head slightly)*
  "Hindi ko kakalimutan 'to."

#### `[BEAT-10.4]` `[Est: 6s]` — The Ghost in the Mist
- **Visual Action:** Wide tracking shot. With a silent, powerful bound over the tall ferns, Kael melts into the dense fog. Two seconds later, the armored silhouettes of Garrick, Brutus, and Rex crash onto the ridge.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Heavy armored boots stomping, Mastiffs panting heavily in fog)*
