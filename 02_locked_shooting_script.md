## Locked Scene & Beat Breakdown

### SCENE 1: ABANDONED RAIL TRANSFER DEPOT — NIGHT (HEAVY RAIN)
**Location:** INT. RAIL TRANSFER DEPOT - GROUND LEVEL & CATWALKS  
**Atmosphere:** Freezing torrential rain slamming corrugated tin roof, kerosene glow, flickering CRT green phosphor, hanging coal soot, infrared lasers.

#### `[BEAT-01.1]` `[Est: 6s]` — The Trembling Drifter
- **Visual Action:** Freezing rain cascades through rusted roof fissures. Elias Reyes shivers on an overturned pallet in grease-stained canvas jackets, staring hollow-eyed at a cracked tablet playing looped news footage of the Black Ridge facility bounty.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Ambient rain drone and low news broadcast audio cue)*

#### `[BEAT-01.2]` `[Est: 6s]` — Subdermal Filaments
- **Visual Action:** Macro extreme close-up of Elias’s scarred palm as he strips off his leather work glove. Rusted rebar puncture oozes clear lymph; beneath the translucent skin, gossamer silver filaments pulse and twist together with wet clicking micro-contortions.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Wet clicking visceral Foley cue)*

#### `[BEAT-01.3]` `[Est: 5s]` — Elias’s Despairing Prayer
- **Visual Action:** Elias curls his fingers inward into a tight, trembling fist, pressing the wounded hand against his duct-taped ribs while exhaling a ragged cloud of vapor into the lantern light.
- **Dialogue:**  
  **ELIAS**  
  *(despairing horror, hushed ragged whisper, trembling breathy rasp, clenching bleeding fist to chest)*  
  "Just stop. Please... just let it stay dead."

#### `[BEAT-02.1]` `[Est: 6s]` — Catwalk Infiltration
- **Visual Action:** A pneumatic hiss vents into the high rafters. Matte-grey Vanguard PMC operators in non-reflective ceramic plate creep across the skeletal catwalks, painting the oily floor below with crossing infrared targeting lasers.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Pressurized gas hiss and boot clicks on iron grating)*

#### `[BEAT-02.2]` `[Est: 7s]` — Graves Breaches the Depot
- **Visual Action:** Hydraulic cutters slice through the cargo bay deadbolts with a shower of orange sparks. Captain Graves strides through the opening in a tailored tactical trench coat, thumbing the activation switch of a humming neuro-restraint baton as four contractors fan out with magnetic net-launchers.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Low hum of restraint baton capacitors)*

#### `[BEAT-02.3]` `[Est: 7s]` — Graves’s Extraction Directive (Part 1)
- **Visual Action:** Graves steps calmly over rail ties, raising his left hand to tap his tactical throat mic while tracking Elias with cold, clinical eyes.
- **Dialogue:**  
  **GRAVES**  
  *(clinical detachment, measured tactical command, icy modulated baritone, tapping collar comm with gloved finger)*  
  "Visual confirmed on Asset Zero. He’s intact. Disable the extremities."

#### `[BEAT-02.4]` `[Est: 7s]` — Graves’s Extraction Directive (Part 2)
- **Visual Action:** The Vanguard contractors raise pneumatic harpoon launchers, locking optical sights onto Elias’s joints.
- **Dialogue:**  
  **GRAVES**  
  *(uncompromising authority, firm precise cadence, cold corporate voice, sweeping baton toward Elias)*  
  "Do not damage the thoracic cavity; bio-logistics needs the bone marrow uncompromised."

#### `[BEAT-02.5]` `[Est: 7s]` — The Pry Bar Ultimatum
- **Visual Action:** Elias scrambles backward off the pallet, kicking a crate aside and hoisting a rusted three-foot iron pry bar with two hands, knuckles whitening as his eyes dart wildly toward the rafter girders.
- **Dialogue:**  
  **ELIAS**  
  *(cornered fury, ragged projective shout, raspy strained pitch, leveling iron pry-bar at squad)*  
  "Get back! I swear to God, I’ll pull the rafters down!"

#### `[BEAT-02.6]` `[Est: 7s]` — Graves’s Psychological Probe (Part 1)
- **Visual Action:** Graves halts five paces away, unhurried, resting the humming baton against his hip and cocking his head with mocking sympathy.
- **Dialogue:**  
  **GRAVES**  
  *(condescending pity, calm conversational delivery, smooth mocking timbre, tilting head with faint smirk)*  
  "Mr. Reyes, you’ve spent six weeks running on severed tendons and fractured hips."

#### `[BEAT-02.7]` `[Est: 8s]` — Graves’s Psychological Probe (Part 2)
- **Visual Action:** Graves takes one deliberate step closer into the lantern glow, the electric arc flickering blue across his cheekbones.
- **Dialogue:**  
  **GRAVES**  
  *(predatory cruelty, low quiet cadence, silken menacing whisper, taking measured step forward)*  
  "You know as well as I do that pain doesn’t stop you anymore. But it still hurts, doesn’t it?"

#### `[BEAT-03.1]` `[Est: 6s]` — Pneumatic Harpoon Strike
- **Visual Action:** Graves snaps his fingers. Two pneumatic harpoons discharge with sharp concussive cracks, piercing Elias’s right shoulder and deep into his left thigh, driving through meat and pinning deep into the heavy timber floor.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Gunshot-like pneumatic discharge and hollow timber impact)*

#### `[BEAT-03.2]` `[Est: 6s]` — Winch Tension & Elias’s Howl
- **Visual Action:** Elias drops the iron bar, shrieking in unendurable human agony as steel winches spool high-tensile braided cables taut, dragging him hard onto both knees against the wet concrete.
- **Dialogue:**  
  **GRAVES**  
  *(authoritative calm, rapid military command, crisp detached tone, gesturing two fingers forward)*  
  "Tension lines. Secure the secondary clamps."

#### `[BEAT-03.3]` `[Est: 7s]` — The Biomechanical Revolt
- **Visual Action:** Sweat and bile splash from Elias’s lips. Deep inside his sternum, a sound like subterranean river ice cracking shudders outward. The wound around the thigh harpoon flares with bioluminescent violet luminescence as the muscle fibers knot and warp around the barbed steel.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Sub-bass organic crunch and violet photon pulse)*

#### `[BEAT-03.4]` `[Est: 7s]` — The Self-Inflicted Tear
- **Visual Action:** Howling with feral fury, Elias clamps both bare hands around the barbed shaft anchored in his thigh and violently wrenches it backward, ripping the barbed prongs straight out through muscle and skin.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Guttural roar and sickening wet muscle tearing)*

#### `[BEAT-03.5]` `[Est: 7s]` — Cellular Suture & The Defiant Shout
- **Visual Action:** Stunned Vanguard contractors lower their launchers in shock as dense silver filaments erupt across the open wound, pulling the torn flesh together in four seconds. Elias pushes up onto the restored leg, blood dripping from his chin.
- **Dialogue:**  
  **ELIAS**  
  *(feral defiance, breathless gasping shout, blood-choked gravelly timbre, thrusting bloodied chest forward)*  
  "You can’t keep me in a jar!"

#### `[BEAT-04.1]` `[Est: 6s]` — Federal Breach from the Skylights
- **Visual Action:** The high skylights shatter inward with deafening explosions. Thermite canisters and stun grenades plummet into the depot floor, igniting into a blinding white magnesium inferno as blinding flash-bangs detonate in rapid tri-bursts.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Glass shattering and concussive flash-bang detonations)*

#### `[BEAT-04.2]` `[Est: 7s]` — Vance’s Federal Quarantine Ultimatum
- **Visual Action:** Heavy armored Federal Containment operatives fast-rope down into the smoke. Commander Vance touches down, planting his combat boots and leveling a heavy tactical rifle at Graves’s line.
- **Dialogue:**  
  **VANCE**  
  *(commanding tactical authority, amplified speaker broadcast, booming metallic bark, aiming assault rifle at PMC perimeter)*  
  "Vanguard personnel! Step down and surrender the asset! You are in violation of Federal Quarantine Order Nine!"

#### `[BEAT-04.3]` `[Est: 7s]` — Graves’s Corporate Retort
- **Visual Action:** Graves pivots smoothly, drawing a heavy high-caliber sidearm from his coat and aiming it directly at Vance’s biohazard visor, face contorting with rage.
- **Dialogue:**  
  **GRAVES**  
  *(combative fury, snarled tactical command, clipped venomous baritone, cocking high-caliber sidearm at Vance)*  
  "Sovereign Helix holds the exclusive salvage charter on this vector, Commander! Clear the kill zone!"

#### `[BEAT-04.4]` `[Est: 6s]` — The Crossfire Eruption
- **Visual Action:** A Vanguard contractor fires an assault rifle burst up into the ropes. An operative returns fire with an automatic shotgun, obliterating a concrete pillar. Heavy caliber rounds shred iron girders into a storm of sparks.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Automatic crossfire and explosive concrete fragmentation)*

#### `[BEAT-05.1]` `[Est: 6s]` — Trapped in the Meat Grinder
- **Visual Action:** Trapped between both firing lines, Elias is struck across the torso by a stray 5.56mm burst. Three rounds pierce his abdomen; two shatter his right collarbone, hurling him backward through the rotting wooden slats of a decommissioned freight car.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Flesh impacts, wood splintering, and heavy iron floor thud)*

#### `[BEAT-05.2]` `[Est: 6s]` — Clinical Death inside the Rail Car
- **Visual Action:** Inside the darkened freight car, Elias lies faceup on the iron floor plates, choking on dark venous blood. His pupils dilate, his trembling ceases, and his breathing goes utterly still. Outside, the deafening crossfire roars on.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Muffled exterior gunfire and eerie low room tone)*

#### `[BEAT-05.3]` `[Est: 7s]` — The Viral Lattice Defibrillation
- **Visual Action:** Violet bioluminescent traceries erupt through Elias's chest like an electric circuit board. His spine snaps into a violent convulsive arch off the iron floor; entry wounds dilate, forcefully expelling deformed lead slugs straight out of the chest as ribs realign with snapping bone clicks.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Resonant organic defibrillator crack and lead slugs clinking on iron)*

#### `[BEAT-05.4]` `[Est: 6s]` — The Bile Heave
- **Visual Action:** Elias collapses back onto all fours, coughing violently as he vomits thick black viral bile and copper-tasting blood onto the floorplates, gasping ragged gulps of dust-choked air.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Wet retching and ragged convulsive gasping)*

### SCENE 2: SUBTERRANEAN STORM DRAINAGE CULVERT — NIGHT
**Location:** INT. MUNICIPAL DRAINAGE CULVERT & OUTFLOW GRATE  
**Atmosphere:** Pitch-black concrete tunnel, rushing freezing storm runoff, echo of distant city sirens, reflections of metropolitan neon through iron outflow bars.

#### `[BEAT-06.1]` `[Est: 6s]` — The Standstill & Clearing Smoke
- **Visual Action:** The firefight dies down into drifting tear gas and white smoke. Graves clutches a shattered ceramic shoulder plate; Vance limps forward with his rifle raised. Both commanders converge cautiously upon the shattered rail car siding.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Hiss of dying thermite and cautious crunch of combat boots on glass)*

#### `[BEAT-06.2]` `[Est: 6s]` — The Empty Coffin
- **Visual Action:** Vance and Graves raise their tactical weapon lights into the rail car interior. The floor is covered in a pool of black-red blood, six deformed bullets, shredded canvas rags, and a smeared bloody palm-print trailing down into an open floor drainage grate.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Tactical flashlight sweep and dripping water)*

#### `[BEAT-06.3]` `[Est: 6s]` — The Subterranean Runoff
- **Visual Action:** Waist-deep in rushing icy black stormwater, Elias wades through the subterranean drainage tunnel. His skin glows with dying silver filament patterns that fade beneath pale flesh as he staggers through the darkness.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Heavy rushing water surge and ragged, exhausted breathing)*

#### `[BEAT-06.4]` `[Est: 7s]` — The City Outflow Breach
- **Visual Action:** Elias reaches the corroded iron outflow grating. Beyond the bars, miles of rain-swept neon towers and private surveillance beacons pulse across the cityscape. He wraps his bleeding hands around the bars, sets his boots into the gravel silt, and heaves with unnatural power.
- **Dialogue:** *(None / Creak of groaning iron bars and distant urban siren wail)*
