# 01_polished_script.md

## Scene Metadata

* **Title / Scene Name:** The Shape of Home
* **Version:** v2.0 Post-Editorial Revision
* **Location:** INT. MANOY’S APARTMENT — VARIOUS NIGHTS AND DAYS; FINAL NIGHT TO DAWN
* **Characters Present:** Manoy, 26; Aramiya, a holographic companion AI; Lia, Manoy’s sister
* **Language Style:** English narration / natural Taglish dialogue
* **Revision Log:**
  * Segmented the story into short, single-turn clip beats with reaction or visual cutaways between dialogue turns.
  * Separated time jumps, hardware changes, the server discovery, and the final shutdown into distinct visual units.
  * Split long dialogue only at existing sentence boundaries, preserving the wording; restored the source sentence indicated by the editorial note in Aramiya’s reveal.
  * Clarified that the neural band is armed but awaiting consent, then shows an unauthorized connection attempt and its cancellation when Manoy disconnects power.
  * Made Manoy’s closing grief visual rather than internal narration; removed the workflow handoff from story content.
