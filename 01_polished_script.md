**Beat 1: The Broken Fugitive**

Sheets of freezing rain slam against the rusted corrugated siding of an abandoned rail transfer depot. Inside, the only light is the dying orange glow of a kerosene lantern and the phosphor-green flicker of a cracked emergency radio receiver.

ELIAS REYES sits on an overturned wooden pallet, trembling. He wears three layers of grease-stained canvas jackets, duct tape wrapped tight around his ribs. On the floor beside him lies a battery-powered tablet. The cracked screen plays a looped, grainy news feed:

> *"...classified footage leaked from the Black Ridge facility confirms the subject survived catastrophic internal trauma, high-caliber trauma, and full chemical incineration. Sovereign Helix stock surged twelve points following rumors of a nine-figure private recovery bounty..."*

Elias looks down at his hands. He pulls off a blood-crusted leather work glove. Across his palm is a jagged, two-inch puncture from rusted rebar taken an hour ago. Under the dim lantern light, the flesh does not bleed red. It weeps a thick, pale lymph. Beneath the skin, gossamer silver filaments—ancient, algorithmic—writhe and stitch the muscle together with an audible, wet clicking sound.

He squeezes his fist shut. His breath shudders through cracked lips.

ELIAS
(whispering to the empty room)
Just stop. Please... just let it stay dead.

The cellular stitch does not stop. It knits the flesh tight, leaving a faint, petrified silver line across his skin.

---

**Beat 2: The Silent Perimeter**

A rhythmic hiss breaks the silence—pressurized gas.

High up in the depot’s rusted rafters, thin beams of infrared light cut through the hanging soot. Shadows move across the catwalks with unnatural discipline.

Elias’s eyes dart upward. He recognizes the gear before he sees the faces: matte-grey tactical armor, non-reflective ceramic plating, and pneumatic harpoon launchers rather than standard assault rifles. **Helix-Vanguard PMC.**

Down below, at the far end of the warehouse, the roll-up cargo doors groan. Hydraulic cutters chew through the deadbolts.

CAPTAIN GRAVES steps through the opening. He wears a tailored tactical coat over light armor, holding a carbon-fiber neuro-restraint baton that hums with low-frequency current. Four contractors flank him, leveling magnetic net-launchers.

GRAVES
(speaking into a collar mic, calm)
Visual confirmed on Asset Zero. He’s intact. Disable the extremities. Do not damage the thoracic cavity; bio-logistics needs the bone marrow uncompromised.

Elias scrambles to his feet, grabbing a heavy iron pry bar off the concrete.

ELIAS
Get back! I swear to God, I’ll pull the rafters down!

GRAVES
Mr. Reyes, you’ve spent six weeks running on severed tendons and fractured hips. You know as well as I do that pain doesn’t stop you anymore. But it still hurts, doesn’t it?

---

**Beat 3: First Contact & The Cost of Living**

Graves snaps his fingers. Two contractors fire simultaneously.

Compressed-gas harpoons strike Elias: one through his right shoulder, the second dead-center into his thigh. The steel prongs punch straight through muscle and anchor into the timber floor behind him.

Elias screams—a raw, gut-tearing howl. He drops the pry bar, clawing at the cables pinned through his body.

GRAVES
Tension lines. Secure the secondary clamps.

The cables winch taut, dragging Elias to his knees. The agony is absolute. Sweat and bile spill from his mouth. His nervous system screams with full, unmediated human agony. But then the ancient pathogen responds.

Deep in his core, a sound like cracking river ice echoes beneath his sternum. The flesh around the steel shafts flares pale bioluminescent violet. His quadricep muscles hyper-contract, warping around the barbed prongs.

With a feral roar, Elias grips the thigh harpoon with both hands and *yanks*.

He rips the anchor through his own quadricep, tearing flesh and tendon wide open.

The mercenaries freeze in shock. Before the severed muscle can even hang loose, thick silver filaments shoot across the gap, pulling the meat together like an invisible suture. Within four seconds, he is standing on the ruined leg again.

ELIAS
(gasping, blood running from his nostrils)
You can’t keep me in a jar!

---

**Beat 4: The Convergence (State Intervention)**

Before Graves can deploy the cryogenic stun nets, the reinforced skylights above detonate inward.

Stun grenades and thermite canisters crash onto the depot floor, turning the warehouse into a blinding white inferno. Flash-bangs detonate in rapid three-round bursts.

COMMANDER VANCE and a squad of heavily armored Federal Containment operatives breach from the upper gantry, fast-roping down into the center of the depot. Their armor bears government biohazard seals.

VANCE
(amplified through his helmet speaker)
Vanguard personnel! Step down and surrender the asset! You are in violation of Federal Quarantine Order Nine!

GRAVES
(snarls, drawing a high-caliber sidearm)
Sovereign Helix holds the exclusive salvage charter on this vector, Commander! Clear the kill zone!

Panic snaps the line. A Vanguard contractor opens fire on the federal ropes. A federal operative returns fire with an automatic shotgun.

The depot explodes into chaotic, close-quarters crossfire. Rifle rounds ricochet off iron girders; high-explosive rounds shatter concrete pillars.

---

**Beat 5: Caught in the Grind**

Elias is trapped in the meat grinder between two armies.

A burst of 5.56mm rounds from a panicked Vanguard gunner rips across Elias’s torso. Three rounds punch through his stomach; two shatter his collarbone.

The impact throws him backward through the wooden siding of a decommissioned freight car. Elias hits the iron floorboards inside, choking on thick, dark arterial blood. His vision tunnels into blackness. His heart stutters to a complete halt.

*Clinical death: three seconds.*

Around him, gunfire roars as the two factions slaughter each other for the right to own his corpse.

*Six seconds.*

Inside Elias's chest, the primordial virus rejects the cessation of life. The viral lattice violently restarts the cardiac muscle like an internal defibrillator. Elias's back arches off the floor with a violent, snapping convulsion.

His lungs fill with air, expelling shattered bullet fragments straight out of the entry wounds as the muscle walls contract and regenerate. The ribs realign with sickening crunches that sound like dry kindling snapping underfoot.

He rolls over onto all fours, vomiting black bile and copper-tasting blood onto the iron floor.

---

**Beat 6: The Vanishing Specimen**

Outside the rail car, Graves drops a federal operative with a point-blank pistol shot, only to take a shotgun blast to his ceramic shoulder plate from Vance. Both men stagger, bleeding, before realizing the shooting around them has thinned to an eerie, echoing silence.

Smoke and tear gas drift through the ruined warehouse.

Vance raises his rifle, stepping toward the shattered rail car. Graves limps forward from the opposite side, raising his sidearm. Both men stop at the entrance.

The freight car is empty.

On the floor lies a pool of dark blood, surrounded by six mangled, expelled lead slugs and torn pieces of Elias’s canvas coat. A bloody handprint smears across the back exit door, leading directly toward an open, iron drainage grate in the floor.

Down in the subterranean storm drainage culvert, waist-deep in rushing, icy runoff, Elias wades through the pitch darkness.

His clothes are shredded. His skin is mottled with faint, glowing silver traceries that slowly fade back into pale flesh. His breath comes in ragged, exhausted heaves. Every muscle burns with the memory of trauma, but he does not slow down.

Up ahead, through the iron storm outflow bars, the distant neon lights of the metropolitan skyline reflect across the rushing water—millions of streetlights, surveillance towers, and private satellites, all searching for a man who cannot die.

Elias wraps his bleeding hands around the corroded iron bars, plants his feet in the silt, and pulls.
